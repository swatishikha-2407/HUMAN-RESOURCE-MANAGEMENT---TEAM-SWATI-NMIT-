25. PAYROLL

Member 4 owns the payroll functionality.

Employee

VIEW salary

but cannot edit it.

Admin

Admin can:

View payroll for all employees
Update salary structure
Maintain payroll accuracy

If your team wants salary slips, you can add:

salary_slips
----------------
id

But salary slips beyond the general reports requirement are an optional enhancement, not a detailed mandatory schema in the problem statement.
